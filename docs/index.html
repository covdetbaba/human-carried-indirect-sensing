indirect sensing

A person carries a smartwatch and a smartphone across a flexible deck. The model solves the person and the deck together and records what the devices measure.

3The model

Deck
Any flexible deck a person walks on: a floor slab, a footbridge, a walkway. Baseline is a 14 m concrete slab, 3 m wide, 0.2 m thick.
Person
Five masses in three directions. Walking force, step timing and arm swing are included.
Coupling
Person and deck are solved together in every time step.
Devices
A watch on each wrist and a phone on each hip, with sensor noise.
Excitation
Three ambient shakers on the deck, plus the person.
